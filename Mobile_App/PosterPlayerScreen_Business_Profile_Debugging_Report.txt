## 12. Root Cause Summary

**PosterPlayerScreen shows posters from the previous business profile because:**

1. **PRIMARY**: Navigation route parameters (`businessCategory`, `initialBusinessProfile`) override BusinessProfileContext
2. **SECONDARY**: useEffect dependencies include stale route params instead of context
3. **TERTIARY**: No mechanism to reload posters when context changes

### Exact Bug Locations:
- **Line 311**: `businessCategory` extracted from route.params (stale)
- **Line 988**: useEffect depends on `businessCategory` route param
- **Line 1134**: Missing `selectedBusinessProfile` in dependencies
- **Line 456-458**: Route params take precedence over context
