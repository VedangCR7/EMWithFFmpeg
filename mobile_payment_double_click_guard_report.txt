MOBILE PAYMENT DOUBLE CLICK GUARD REPORT
=========================================

DATE: 2026-03-04
PATCH: PRODUCTION SAFE MOBILE PAYMENT DOUBLE CLICK PREVENTION

-----------------------------------------------------

PROBLEM IDENTIFIED
------------------

Multiple rapid taps on the "Pay Now / Subscribe" button were causing:

• Duplicate Razorpay orders to be created
• Multiple PENDING transactions in backend
• Confusing transaction history for users
• Potential double payment scenarios

Users could trigger multiple payment processes before the Razorpay modal opened,
leading to inconsistent payment states.
