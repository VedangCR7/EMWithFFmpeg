## 🔧 **Technical Details**

### API Endpoints
- **Create Autopay:** `POST /api/mobile/subscription/create-autopay`
- **Get Status:** `GET /api/mobile/subscription/status?businessProfileId={id}`

### Response Handling
- **Multiple Structures:** Supports `response.data`, `response.data.data`, `response.data.subscription`
- **Field Extraction:** Tries `subscriptionId`, `razorpaySubscriptionId`, `razorpay_subscription_id`, `id`

### Caching Strategy
- **Status Cache:** 10-second TTL for business profile subscriptions
- **Cache Key:** `subscription_status_profile_{businessProfileId}`
- **Stale Data:** Allowed for better UX
