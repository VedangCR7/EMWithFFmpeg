## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**CRITICAL FINDING:** Razorpay Checkout is NOT opening due to a **DATA EXTRACTION MISMATCH** between the backend response format and the frontend expectations. The flow breaks at the subscription ID validation step, preventing Razorpay checkout from ever being reached.
